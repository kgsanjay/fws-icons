Use square viewBox="0 0 128 128", fill="none", a consistent 2.5-unit stroke-width with round caps/joins, and only #202123 and #C31E25. Use simple paths, circles, rectangles and symmetric curves. No CSS, transforms, external links, scripts, text, numbers, logos, white fills, gradients or shadows. Use filled dots only for small functional details. Keep generous transparent margins and clear spacing; prevent overlapping contours and crowded joins. Inspect at 48, 64 and 256 pixels; mathematical stroke consistency alone does not prove optical quality.
